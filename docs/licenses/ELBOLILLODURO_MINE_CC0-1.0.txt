The official Mine page describes a small modular mine model pack and states that
its models are CC0. It also identifies some textures as obtained from
texturer.com and textures.com without providing separate redistribution terms.

- Uploaded archive: Mine.rar
- Uploaded archive SHA-256: 6031036af83452bd85d3bd5047762b672bb49a4d1970a6d94525422055843571
- Imported runtime assets: game/art/models/third_party/elbolilloduro_mine/
- Imported geometry: texture-free derived COLLADA files mine_props.dae and
  mine_modular.dae, preserving the source scene/node names.
- Source FBX files and all source texture files are intentionally omitted from
  the project. The derived DAE files remove image libraries, texture bindings,
  and external texture paths, then use neutral material colors for review.
- Reusable prop examples include barrels, wooden boxes, lockers, a generator,
  medical box, petrol can, shovel, tray, wagon, lamps, fences, rocks, cables,
  TNT, detonator, and elevator/rail components.
- Review scene: game/world/test_scenes/assorted_props_demo.tscn
