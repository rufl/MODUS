# Code of Conduct

> **Documentation status: maintained reference.** This policy governs participation in current project spaces.

MODUS welcomes contributors and players of every background, identity, experience level, and ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume honest mistakes while accepting correction.
- Make room for newcomers and different communication styles.
- Respect privacy, consent, attribution, and accessibility needs.
- Stop when someone asks you to end an unwanted interaction.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, impersonation, spam, and sustained disruption are not acceptable in project spaces. Publishing another person's private information without permission is prohibited.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, review threads, and other spaces where someone represents MODUS.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions, lock discussions, reject participation, or report an account to GitHub when behavior violates this policy. Decisions will consider context, impact, and repeated behavior rather than status or seniority.

Use GitHub's **Report content** flow for abuse or safety concerns. For project-specific moderation, use the contact options on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/rufl). Reports will be handled as privately as the available channel permits. Do not retaliate against anyone who reports a concern in good faith.

This policy draws from the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
